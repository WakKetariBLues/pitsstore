1. Overview

This app ("the App") is a private content management tool used by its owner to publish short product review and promotion videos (books and other products, including affiliate products) to the owner's own TikTok account and to view the performance of those videos. This policy explains what data the App uses.

2. Data we access from TikTok

With your permission through TikTok Login Kit, the App may access:

  • Basic profile info (display name, avatar, open ID)
  • Profile statistics (follower count, likes count, video count)
  • Your public videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares)
  • Permission to upload or publish videos you choose to your own account

3. How we use the data

The data is used only to:

  • Publish videos you select to your TikTok account
  • Show you reports on how your videos and promoted products perform

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising targeting.

4. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect payment details, buyer information or order data. Any purchases made through product links are handled by the seller or platform (for example TikTok Shop) under their own privacy policies.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens and video statistics are stored privately on the owner's own computer and private Google Drive. They are not published or shared.

6. Data retention and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions). On request, all stored data related to your account will be deleted.

7. Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 18.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available at this page.
